About this recording & controls

An empty room is maintained by the people watching it. Comments assign relationships to five stable seats. Hide them and the room forgets. Each two-minute loop returns to the same authored state; your comments return at the time you left them.

Use the timeline or chapter buttons to seek. Select a marked comment in the log, or a moving relationship, to hold it. Space operates focused buttons; arrow keys adjust the timeline. Still mode stops camera drift and interference and fixes comments in readable lanes. Low power caps rendering at 24 fps and 640 pixels wide. Sound starts only when enabled.
